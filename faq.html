Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about the learning tools, navigation, and the project team.

What is this website for?

This site is designed to help students practice basic Japanese vocabulary through flashcards, quizzes, and short video lessons.

How do I use the flashcards?

Choose a vocabulary category, review the word and reading, then click the flip or reveal button to check the meaning.

How does the quiz work?

The quiz shows a Japanese word and asks you to choose the correct English meaning from the options provided.

Can I watch video lessons?

Yes. The Videos page contains themed lesson pages for greetings, food, and animals.

Where can I find the group members?

Visit the About page to read the introduction and placeholder bios for all group members.

Can I add my own pictures?

Yes. The About page includes photo placeholder boxes that you can replace with your own images.
